=============================================================================

This document summarises what each user role can see and do inside the
TTMS (Technology Transfer Management System) internal portal at NSU TTO.

=============================================================================
1. SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR
   Login : admin@tto.northsouth.edu
   Pass  : Admin@TTO2026
=============================================================================

Who they are:
  • The highest-privilege account — typically the IT / portal administrator
    of the Technology Transfer Office.

What they can do:
  • Full access to every section of the portal — all sidebar menu items visible.
  • Dashboard: all-system statistics (total disclosures, pending reviews,
    active patents, cumulative revenue earned) + upcoming patent deadlines.
  • Disclosures: view, review, and manage every disclosure regardless of who
    submitted it; update status at any stage; assign reviewers or officers;
    add reviewer notes and rejection reasons.
  • Patents: create, edit, and view all patent records; manage filing
    deadlines with due-date reminders; track jurisdiction and attorney details.
  • IP Assignments: record and view all IP ownership determinations
    (University / Inventor / Joint / Sponsored Research).
  • Agreements: create, edit, view, and download all legal agreement types
    (assignment, NDA/CDA, revenue sharing, licensing, sponsored research).
  • Commercialization: create and manage all commercialization pipelines
    (licensing, startup spin-off, joint development, direct sale).
  • Revenue: record incoming licence fees and royalties; set up distribution
    splits among inventors, departments, and the university; mark paid.
  • Reports: generate and export Disclosure, Patent, Revenue, and
    Commercialization reports as CSV files.
  • Admin — Users: the ONLY role that can list all portal users, change their
    role, department, designation, or activate / deactivate their account.
  • Admin — Technologies: manage the Technology Portfolio shown on the
    public-facing TTO website.
  • Admin — Startups: manage the Startups directory shown on the public website.

   Login : tto.officer@tto.northsouth.edu
   Pass  : Test@1234
=============================================================================

Who they are:
  • The day-to-day operator of the TTO portal — handles the full IP lifecycle
    from disclosure intake through to commercialisation and revenue.

What they can do:
  • Dashboard: all-system statistics + upcoming patent deadlines.
  • Disclosures: view all disclosures; update status at any stage; assign to

    joint development, direct sale; record partner details and progress.
  • Revenue: record incoming payments; set up distribution splits among
    inventors, departments, and the university; mark individual distributions
    as paid with payment dates.
  • Reports: generate and export all reports.
  • Admin — Technologies & Startups: manage public-facing website listings.
  • Cannot: manage user accounts.

6. FACULTY  (Faculty Inventor)
   Login : faculty@northsouth.edu
   Pass  : Test@1234
=============================================================================

Who they are:
  • A faculty member at NSU who is an inventor and wishes to disclose
    intellectual property created through research or teaching activities.

What they can do:
  • Dashboard: personal stats only — total disclosures submitted, drafts in
    progress.
  • Disclosures — own submissions only:
    - Fill and save an invention disclosure form as a draft at any time.
    - Add co-inventors (internal portal users or external collaborators) with
      name, email, department, and designation.
    - Upload supporting documents (drawings, experimental data, forms) in
      PDF, Word, or image formats.
    - Edit and update the draft any number of times before submission.
    - Click "Submit to TTO" to formally submit — the system assigns a unique
      DISC-YYYY-NNNN reference number and locks the form from further editing.
    - Track the status of each submission through the full review pipeline:
        Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Ownership Determined
        → Patentability Assessed → Committee Review
        → Approved / Rejected → Patent Filing → Commercializing
  • Cannot see other inventors' disclosures.
  • Cannot access: Patents, Assignments, Agreements, Commercialization,
    Revenue, Reports, Admin panels.

DISCLOSURE STATUS PIPELINE  (for reference during walkthrough)
=============================================================================

  draft               → Inventor is filling in the form; not yet submitted.
  submitted           → Formally submitted to TTO; awaiting assignment.
  under_review        → Assigned reviewer is evaluating the disclosure.
  ownership_determined→ IP ownership (University / Inventor / Joint) decided.
  patentability_assessed → Patentability search and opinion completed.
  committee_review    → TTO committee is deliberating on the next action.
  approved            → Disclosure approved for patent filing / licensing.
  rejected            → Disclosure rejected (reason recorded in the system).
  patent_filing       → Patent application in progress at the IP office.
  commercializing     → Active licensing, spin-off, or commercialization track.
